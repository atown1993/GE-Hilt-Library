# Contributors

This catalog grows past what any one person can capture. Everyone who
contributes a firsthand capture of their own hilt is credited here, by the name
or handle they ask to be credited under.

| Contributor | Hilts contributed | First contribution |
|---|---|---|
| atown1993 (maintainer) | Savi's Workshop (8 kyber colors), plus Legacy hilts — see `captures/` | project start |

Want to be on this list? See `CONTRIBUTING.md`. You send a best-effort raw
capture of your hilt; the maintainer does the decoding and normalization and
adds you here.
